How an evening goes

Ten minutes or forty. The phone just holds the questions.

One of you reads aloud, the other answers, and the phone can sit on the table while you talk. There’s no timer, no score and nothing to fill in.

Private by design

What you say tonight stays between the two of you.

No InnerUs account. Processing on your device. Optional sync to your private iCloud.

  • Recording only when you tap record

    One answer at a time. On supported iPhones, speech can be transcribed on the phone, without a cloud fallback.

  • Your moments, behind Face ID

    Lock the app with Face ID or your passcode. Notifications never show what you wrote.

  • We never receive it

    No InnerUs server for personal content, no app analytics. When iCloud sync is enabled, selected moments can also be stored in your private iCloud.

Questions

Good to know

Something else? Visit support or write to us.

Do we both need the app?

No. One phone is enough. One of you reads the question aloud and you talk. InnerUs is built so the phone can rest on the table.

Is this relationship therapy?

No. InnerUs is for couples who enjoy each other and want more than logistics. It doesn’t score, test or diagnose anything, and either of you can skip a question or stop at any time.

Is there a subscription?

No. Start a free week whenever you like. When it ends nothing is charged; you keep the six free conversations and can buy the collection once.

Can we use it in Dutch?

Yes. Every conversation is available in English and Nederlands. InnerUs follows your iPhone’s language, and you can switch in Settings.

Is it on Android?

The new InnerUs is being prepared for iPhone first. Android plans are still being worked out.

## Description  (2391/4000)

InnerUs gives the two of you a conversation for tonight. Put the phone between you, read the question out loud and talk. A conversation takes about ten minutes, and nothing counts down.

HOW IT WORKS
• Start the conversation that is ready on Tonight, or pick one yourself.
• Five questions build like a story, illustrated in watercolour.
• Stuck? Open a nudge for an angle or a few examples. Or skip the question.
• A closing question asks what you want to remember.
• No time for a whole conversation? Ask for one quick question.

WHAT YOU TALK ABOUT
18 conversations in three themes:
• Our story: your first date, a trip you still talk about, a hard season you got through together.
• Us today: an ordinary Tuesday, the things that make you laugh, what money means to each of you.
• What's next: a Saturday of your own, a dream you keep postponing, the next chapter you hope for.

The questions ask about real things: a day, a place, a habit, something one of you said. They never assume how long you have been together, whether you are married or whether you have children. There are no scores, no tests and no streaks. InnerUs is not therapy. It is a good reason to talk.

KEEP WHAT MATTERS (OPTIONAL)
Save a moment from any conversation: a typed note, or a voice recording of an answer. On supported iPhones, InnerUs can turn a recording into text and suggest a short summary, entirely on your iPhone. You check it before anything is saved. On other iPhones you simply write the note yourself.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-in, no email address.
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
• Everything stays on your iPhone. We run no server that stores your conversations.
• Optional sync through your own private iCloud.
• Lock the app with Face ID.
• Export everything, or delete it all, whenever you want.

WHAT IT COSTS
• Six conversations are free for good, including Our first date, An ordinary Tuesday and A Saturday of our own. Quick questions are free too.
• Want to try the rest? Start your free week with one tap. All 18 conversations open, nothing is charged and the week ends by itself.
• Keep the other 12 with one payment of €9.99 (or the equivalent in your currency). No subscription. Nothing renews.
• Recording, Moments, iCloud sync and export are never behind a payment.

## Category and age rating

- Primary: **Lifestyle** · Secondary: **Entertainment**. Lifestyle is where couple and relationship apps are browsed and fits a calm evening ritual. Health & Fitness is the usual alternative (Paired, Lasting), but it frames the app as wellness/therapy, which InnerUs explicitly is not. Entertainment as secondary matches conversation cards and quick questions without that framing. If Nick wants maximum browse exposure next to Paired, swap the secondary to Health & Fitness.
- Age rating: **4+**. Questions are about dates, trips, habits and plans; the most intimate words are “laugh, or blush”. No romance-explicit, sexual, alcohol or medical content, no sharing, no web view. The questionnaire therefore yields 4+. Optional: Apple lets you set a higher minimum age; 13+ would signal the adult-couple audience but narrows Family Sharing reach. Recommended: keep 4+.
- Google Play: Everyone / PEGI 3 / USK 0 expected (no violence, sexual content, gambling, user interaction or location sharing).

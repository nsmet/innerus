# Website and App Store review — InnerUs 2.0

Reviewed against the sources below on 29 October 2026. This is a release checklist, not evidence that Apple has approved the app or that the legal documents are complete. The website describes the planned 2.0 release, while the earlier version may still be in use. Public URLs are not yet hosted.

## Apple requirements and app configuration

- [ ] Publish a publicly reachable privacy policy at `https://innerus.app/privacy.html`; verify both that App Store Connect has that URL and that the app's Settings link opens it. Apple requires both links and a policy explaining collection, use, third parties, retention and deletion. Check the English and `#nl` destinations from the actual build. [App Review 5.1.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
- [ ] Publish `https://innerus.app/support.html`, with `info@innerus.app` functioning and monitored. Apple requires a support URL leading to actual contact details; whether additional address/telephone disclosures are required depends on applicable law. [Platform version information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information/).
- [ ] Ensure App Store Connect's App Privacy answers reflect the shipped app and third-party SDKs across platforms, including the still-live Android version if it shares the same app record. Do not select “Data Not Collected” solely on the basis of the iOS v2 design. [Manage app privacy](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-privacy/).
- [ ] Confirm whether the developer is a trader under the EU Digital Services Act and provide the applicable details in App Store Connect. Nick prefers not to publish a street address on the site, but legal and Apple requirements may require address/contact disclosure depending on circumstances; do not assume that a town or country suffices. [App information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information/).
- [ ] Confirm the actual App Store licence setting. Apple applies its standard EULA unless a custom EULA is selected; the site terms are supplemental and should not be entered as a custom EULA without review. [Custom licence](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/provide-a-custom-license-agreement/).
- [ ] Verify store products, trial, prices, legacy entitlements, cancellation and refund instructions against the final release. Check app screenshots, artwork rights and accessibility, and remove the in-development wording when appropriate. Ensure all links are live and free of temporary or placeholder content before submission. [App Review](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/).

## Privacy and legal decisions still needed

- [ ] Nick has requested deletion of old data. Do not claim it has been deleted until the live Firebase Auth/Firestore and other legacy service inventories, deletion scope, timing, backups, legal obligations, and any still-active Android v1 dependency are checked. Do not delete production data or turn off services as part of this website task. Determine what applies to Mixpanel, Qualli, Firebase and RevenueCat, and preserve appropriate records of what was actually done.
- [ ] Confirm the email provider, hosting/CDN and any security or analytics settings, cookies, actual technical logging, recipients/subprocessors, retention periods or objective criteria, and where data is processed. Confirm international transfer mechanisms and processor agreements where applicable. Do not assume Cloudflare is configured or that every provider provides a particular safeguard. [European Commission privacy information](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-individuals_en); [EDPB international transfers](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/international-data-transfers_en).
- [ ] Confirm the applicable legal bases for each actual activity, including the earlier version, website traffic and support. The present wording identifies possible bases but is not a completed processing inventory. Confirm contact details, any required public trader identification and the scope of distribution.
- [ ] Verify deletion/export/backup, local processing, iCloud defaults and scope, purchase behavior and privacy claims on real devices and configured services. The copy is grounded in intended behavior; several release-gate checks remain open.
- [ ] Have a qualified reviewer assess the bilingual privacy policy and terms against the final facts and relevant jurisdictions. The policy currently identifies unresolved provider and transfer details explicitly; finalize them rather than presenting assumptions as facts. Do not claim that digital-content withdrawal rights automatically disappear: the outcome depends on applicable law and the required prior consent and acknowledgment. [EU consumer guidance](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/returns/indexamp_en.htm).
